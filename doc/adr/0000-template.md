How to use: copy this file to `NNNN-short-title.md` with the next free number, fill it in, and add a row to the [index](README.md). Keep it short: one of us must be able to answer a defense question from it in under a minute. Lines marked **Fact:** link their source; everything else is our own reasoning.

- One reason per bullet.
- **Fact:** a claim taken from documentation. ([Source title](https://example.com))
- Measurements from our cluster, with the numbers and how we got them.

## Trade-offs

- What it costs us, its known limits, and why we accept them.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Option:** why not, in one or two lines.

Optional: the event that would make us change this decision.
